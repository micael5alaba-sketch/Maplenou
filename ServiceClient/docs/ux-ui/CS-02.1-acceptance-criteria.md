# CS-02.1 — Acceptance Criteria

- **AC-UX-001:** Customer, seller, or delivery driver can reach ticket creation after authentication.
- **AC-UX-002:** Successful creation represents the ticket as **NEW**.
- **AC-UX-003:** Requester receives the ticket number.
- **AC-UX-004:** Ticket UI clearly communicates current lifecycle status.
- **AC-UX-005:** Authorized support personnel can understand the support queue.
- **AC-UX-006:** Internal notes are visually and functionally distinct from customer-visible messages.
- **AC-UX-007:** Presented SLA information follows CS-01 targets and business-hour rules.
- **AC-UX-008:** UX does not imply that a requester can force Critical priority.
- **AC-UX-009:** Upload UI communicates approved formats and limits before submission.
- **AC-UX-010:** RESOLVED exposes requester reopening request; CLOSED does not expose requester reopening.
- **AC-UX-011:** Actions are enabled/displayed according to authorization.
- **AC-UX-012:** Successful and failed actions provide clear, timely feedback.
- **AC-UX-013:** Design follows WCAG 2.2 Level AA as the accessibility baseline.
- **AC-UX-014:** Core ticket creation and follow-up journeys remain usable on desktop and mobile.
