## Key retained rules

New tickets start `NEW`. Critical priority is restricted to authorized support roles. Attachments remain 10 MB/file, 5 files/message, 50 MB/ticket. Internal notes are support-only. Reopening applies to `RESOLVED` with support approval; `CLOSED` cannot be requester-reopened. SLA visibility includes first-response and resolution state. Notification failure must not corrupt ticket state. Authorization remains resource/action-level.

## Technology boundary

The visual reference is a UX baseline only. Any Next.js, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, Firebase, or other stack choice remains subject to CS-03 technical planning.
