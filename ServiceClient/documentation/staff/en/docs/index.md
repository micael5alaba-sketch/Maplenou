# Internal support guide

This guide explains how to handle support requests from Maplenou customers, sellers and delivery drivers in the support area of the customer-service application: the queue, handling a ticket, SLA targets, escalation, reopening, administration and reports.

!!! warning "Internal site"
    This site is reserved for Maplenou support staff. It describes internal rules, information visible only to support (internal notes, SLA, payment and delivery context) and the rights of each role. Do not share it with requesters: they have their own user guide.

## Who this guide is for

| Audience | Role in the application | What matters most to you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Support agents | <span class="ui">Support agent</span> | [Daily work](daily-work.md), [Handle a ticket](handle-ticket.md), [Statuses and transitions](statuses-and-transitions.md), [SLA and calendar](sla.md), [Good practices](good-practices.md) |
| Support managers | <span class="ui">Support manager</span> | In addition: [Escalation](escalation.md), [Reopening](reopening.md), reassignment ([Roles and access](roles-and-access.md)), [Reports](reports.md) |
| Administrators | <span class="ui">Administrator</span> | In addition: [Administration](administration.md) (agent activation, support roles, import of former tickets, categories, SLA calendar) |

Requesters (customers, sellers, delivery drivers) create tickets from their own area; they never see this guide or the information reserved for support.

## Find an answer quickly

- **Search**: press the <kbd>/</kbd> key (or click the magnifying glass at the top of the page) and type a few words, for example "reopening" or "Critical". Results point to the exact section.
- **Assistant**: click the <span class="ui">Ask a question</span> button at the bottom of the page and ask your question in plain language ("What are the SLA targets?", "When should I escalate a ticket?"). The assistant answers from this guide only and cites its references: click a reference to open the matching section and check.
- **From the application**: the <span class="ui">Support guide</span> link in the side menu of the support area opens this guide in a new tab.

!!! tip "Switching language"
    The language selector at the top of the page switches between French and English while staying on the same page.

## Where the rules come from

The rules described here come from the module's approved business rules (identifiers `BR-xxx`), the approved decisions `DEC-001` to `DEC-007` and the actual behaviour of the application. When a rule has not yet been approved by the project owner, the page says so in a "pending approval" box. If you are unsure about a rule, ask your support manager rather than improvising.
