# Getting started

This page explains how to open the customer service app, sign in and find your way around the screens.

## Open the customer service app

Maplenou customer service works in a web browser, on a computer or a phone. Open the app address that Maplenou gave you.

Maplenou mobile apps may also give access to customer service. Everything described in this guide works the same way.

## Sign in with your Maplenou account

Only signed-in users can create a ticket. You use your usual **Maplenou account**: the same phone number and password as in the Maplenou app. There is no separate account for customer service.

1. On the <span class="ui">Sign in</span> page, enter your <span class="ui">Phone number</span> with the country code, for example +228 90 00 00 00.
2. Enter your <span class="ui">Password</span>, then click <span class="ui">Sign in</span>.
3. If two-step verification is turned on for your account, the <span class="ui">Two-step verification</span> screen appears. Enter the 6-digit <span class="ui">Verification code</span> shown by your authenticator app, then click <span class="ui">Confirm</span>. The <span class="ui">Back</span> button returns to the password step.
4. The help screen opens.

Your name, your profile (customer, seller or delivery driver) and your contact details come from your Maplenou account. Support therefore knows who you are: you do not need to say so in your tickets.

!!! warning "Session expired"
    After a while, your session ends. The sign-in screen then shows the message <span class="ui">Your session has expired. Please sign in again.</span> Sign in again to continue.

## Solve a sign-in problem

| Message shown | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| <span class="ui">Wrong phone number or password.</span> | Check the number (with the country code) and the password: they are the ones of the Maplenou app. |
| <span class="ui">Wrong or expired verification code.</span> | Enter the new code shown by your authenticator app. |
| <span class="ui">The verification expired. Please sign in again.</span> | Click <span class="ui">Back</span> and enter your password again. |
| <span class="ui">Too many attempts. Try again in a few minutes.</span> | Wait a few minutes before trying again. |
| <span class="ui">This account is disabled. Contact Maplenou support.</span> | Your Maplenou account cannot sign in: contact Maplenou. |
| <span class="ui">The Maplenou sign-in service is temporarily unavailable. Please try again.</span> | Try again a little later. |

**Forgot your password?** Customer service cannot change it: reset it from the Maplenou app, then sign in here with the new password.

## Sign in to the demo version

The demo version lets you explore customer service with sample data. It shows the <span class="ui">Demo sign-in</span> page instead of the Maplenou sign-in.

1. Open the demo app.
2. In the <span class="ui">Requesters (customer app, seller, delivery driver)</span> section, click a profile.
3. The help screen for that profile opens.

Each profile shows its name and role (<span class="ui">Customer</span>, <span class="ui">Seller</span> or <span class="ui">Delivery driver</span>). To try another role, sign out and choose another profile.

## Discover the help screen

After signing in, you arrive on the help screen. Its title depends on your profile:

| Profile | Screen title | Main button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer | <span class="ui">Help and support</span> | <span class="ui">Create a ticket</span> |
| Seller | <span class="ui">Seller support</span> | <span class="ui">Create a ticket</span> |
| Delivery driver | <span class="ui">Delivery support</span> | <span class="ui">Report an incident</span> |

The screen contains:

- a welcome message with your name;
- the button to **create a ticket** (or **report an incident** for a delivery driver);
- the <span class="ui">My tickets</span> button to see all your requests;
- the <span class="ui">Open requests</span> block, with your latest open tickets and the <span class="ui">See all</span> link (it only appears if you have open tickets);
- the list of <span class="ui">Categories</span>: click a category to create a ticket with that category already selected.

## Move around with the navigation bar

The navigation bar at the bottom of the screen is always visible. It has four entries:

| Entry | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| <span class="ui">Help</span> | The help screen and ticket creation |
| <span class="ui">My tickets</span> | The list of your tickets and their details |
| <span class="ui">Notifications</span> | Your notifications; a red counter shows how many are unread |
| <span class="ui">Account</span> | Your details, your preferences and signing out |

The entry for the page you are on is highlighted.

## Use the header on a large screen

On a computer or a tablet, the header at the top of the screen contains:

- the <span class="ui">Help centre</span> link, which opens this guide in a new tab;
- the language choice (`FR` or `EN`);
- the theme choice;
- your name;
- the <span class="ui">Sign out</span> button.

On a phone, the header is reduced: it only keeps the logo and the <span class="ui">Sign out</span> button. The language, the theme and the link to this guide are then on the <span class="ui">Account</span> page.

## Change the language and theme

The app is available in French and English. It offers three themes: <span class="ui">System</span> (same as your device), <span class="ui">Light</span> and <span class="ui">Dark</span>.

- **On a large screen**: use the two lists in the header.
- **On a phone**: open <span class="ui">Account</span>, then the <span class="ui">Preferences</span> section, and choose the <span class="ui">Language</span> and the <span class="ui">Theme</span>.
- **Before signing in**: the sign-in page (like the demo sign-in page) also offers both choices.

The change is immediate. Your choice is remembered in this browser. See [Account and preferences](account-and-preferences.md) for details.
