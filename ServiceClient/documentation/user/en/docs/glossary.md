# Glossary

The words used in Maplenou customer service and in this guide, explained simply.

## Terms about tickets and people

Ticket
:   Your request for help to Maplenou support: a question, an incident or a problem. Each ticket has a unique number.

Ticket number
:   The identifier of your ticket, for example `TKT-2026-00125`. It is unique and never reused. Give it when you talk to support about this ticket.

Requester
:   The person who creates the ticket: a Maplenou customer, seller or delivery driver.

Customer
:   A person who buys on the Maplenou marketplace.

Seller
:   A person or a business that sells on the Maplenou marketplace.

Delivery driver
:   A person who delivers Maplenou orders.

Support agent
:   A member of the Maplenou support team who handles tickets and replies to you.

Handled by
:   The name of the support agent your ticket is assigned to. Until an agent is assigned, the ticket shows <span class="ui">Awaiting assignment</span>.

Category
:   The type of request: Order, Payment, Delivery, Seller, Account, Return/Refund or Other. A ticket has one category.

Order or delivery reference
:   The order (<span class="ui">Related order</span>) or the delivery (<span class="ui">Related delivery</span>) linked to the ticket. It is optional and must be one of your own orders or deliveries.

## Terms about priority and response times

Priority
:   How urgent the ticket is: <span class="ui">Low</span>, <span class="ui">Medium</span>, <span class="ui">High</span> or <span class="ui">Critical</span>. You choose between <span class="ui">Low</span>, <span class="ui">Medium</span> and <span class="ui">High</span>; support can change it.

Critical
:   The highest priority. It is reserved for support, who give it for example for fraud, a security issue or a serious financial problem.

This is urgent
:   The tick box that tells support your request is serious. It does not make the ticket critical automatically: support decides.

SLA
:   Short for *Service Level Agreement*. These are the times support sets itself to reply to your ticket and to resolve it. They depend on the priority and are counted every day, 24 hours a day and 7 days a week, weekends and public holidays included.

## Terms about statuses

Status
:   The stage your ticket has reached. Support moves it forward.

New
:   The ticket has just been created and is not assigned to an agent yet.

Assigned
:   A support agent has taken on the ticket.

In progress
:   Support is working on your request.

Waiting for customer
:   Support is waiting for information from you. Reply in the conversation so work can resume.

Resolved
:   Support considers the problem solved. You can still request reopening.

Closed
:   The ticket is permanently finished. It can no longer be reopened: create a new ticket if needed.

## Terms about messages and tracking

Conversation
:   The messages between you and support in a ticket. Your description is the first message.

Attachment
:   A file added to a ticket or a message: JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP or PDF, 10 MB maximum per file.

Ticket history
:   The dated list of your ticket's important steps: creation, status changes, messages, reopening.

Notification
:   An alert that tells you about an event on one of your tickets, for example a new reply from support.

Reopening request
:   Your request for support to take up again a <span class="ui">Resolved</span> ticket whose problem is not fixed. Support approves or refuses it.

Resolution
:   The stage where support considers the problem solved: the ticket becomes <span class="ui">Resolved</span>.

Closure
:   The final stage of a ticket: the ticket becomes <span class="ui">Closed</span>.

## Terms about the app

Maplenou account
:   The account you sign in with: the same phone number and password as in the Maplenou app. Your name, your profile and your contact details come from it.

Preferences
:   The app's language and theme, on the <span class="ui">Account</span> page.

Theme
:   The app's colours: <span class="ui">System</span>, <span class="ui">Light</span> or <span class="ui">Dark</span>.

Help centre
:   This guide. The <span class="ui">Help centre</span> link in the app opens it in a new tab.

FCFA
:   The CFA franc, the currency used for order amounts.
