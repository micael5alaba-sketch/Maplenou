# FAQ

Answers to the most common questions about Maplenou customer service. Click a question to show the answer.

## Questions about creating a ticket

??? question "My order does not appear in the “Related order” list. What should I do?"
    The list only shows orders linked to your account. Leave <span class="ui">No order</span>, send the ticket, and write the order reference in the description. Support will be able to find it. See [Give the related order or delivery](create-ticket.md#give-the-related-order-or-delivery).

??? question "Can I choose the Critical priority?"
    No. The <span class="ui">Critical</span> priority is reserved for support. For fraud, a security issue or a serious financial problem, tick <span class="ui">This is urgent</span>: support will consider whether to give your ticket <span class="ui">Critical</span> priority. Ticking this box does not make the ticket critical automatically.

??? question "Which files can I attach?"
    JPG, JPEG, PNG or WEBP images and PDF documents. Each file can be up to 10 MB, with up to 5 files per send and up to 50 MB for all the attachments of a ticket. See [Attach files to the ticket](create-ticket.md#attachments).

??? question "Can I edit or delete a ticket after sending it?"
    No. The subject and description cannot be changed after sending, and a ticket cannot be deleted. To correct or complete your request, write a message in the ticket's conversation.

## Questions about following a ticket

??? question "How do I know if support has replied?"
    You receive the <span class="ui">New reply</span> notification, and the red counter on the <span class="ui">Notifications</span> entry goes up. You can also open the ticket in <span class="ui">My tickets</span> and read the conversation. See [Notifications](notifications.md).

??? question "When will support reply?"
    Support's response times are counted every day, 24 hours a day and 7 days a week, weekends and public holidays included. They depend on the ticket's priority: the higher the priority, the faster the reply.

??? question "My ticket says “Waiting for customer”. What should I do?"
    Support is waiting for information from you. Read their latest message, then reply in the conversation. As soon as you reply, the ticket goes back <span class="ui">In progress</span>. See [Reply when support asks for information](follow-tickets.md#reply-when-support-asks-for-information).

??? question "Who can see my ticket?"
    You and the Maplenou support team. Other customers, sellers and delivery drivers cannot see your tickets or your attachments.

## Questions about reopening and your account

??? question "My ticket is resolved, but the problem is not fixed. What should I do?"
    Open the ticket, click <span class="ui">Request reopening</span> and explain why the problem is not resolved. Support reviews your request and tells you their decision. See [Reopening and closing](reopen-and-close.md).

??? question "Can I reopen a closed ticket?"
    No. A <span class="ui">Closed</span> ticket can no longer be reopened. Create a new ticket with the <span class="ui">New ticket</span> button, and give the number of the old ticket in the description.

??? question "How do I change my name, email or phone number?"
    This information comes from your Maplenou account and cannot be changed in customer service. Update your Maplenou account. See [Account and preferences](account-and-preferences.md).

??? question "Which account do I sign in with?"
    With your Maplenou account: the same phone number and password as in the Maplenou app. If two-step verification is on, also enter the 6-digit code of your authenticator app. See [Sign in with your Maplenou account](getting-started.md#sign-in-with-your-maplenou-account).

??? question "I forgot my password. What should I do?"
    Reset it from the Maplenou app, then sign in to customer service with the new password. Customer service cannot change your password.

??? question "Why am I back on the sign-in screen?"
    Your session has expired, or you signed out. The message <span class="ui">Your session has expired. Please sign in again.</span> then appears. Sign in again: your tickets are kept.
